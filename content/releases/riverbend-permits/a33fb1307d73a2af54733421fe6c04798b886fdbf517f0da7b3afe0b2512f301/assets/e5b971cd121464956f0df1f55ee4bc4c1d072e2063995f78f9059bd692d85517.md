# Riverbend Mobile Food Vending Ordinance as amended by Draft Amendment 2 (fictional)

This consolidated text shows how the fictional ordinance would read if the fictional Draft Amendment 2 were adopted. It is invented for the Law Machine demonstration and is not real law.

Section 1. Applications.
(a) A person who wishes to operate a mobile food vehicle shall apply to the Licensing Office.
(b) The Licensing Office shall decide a complete application within 10 business days after it is filed.
(c) If the Licensing Office does not decide a complete application within the period in subsection (b), the application is deemed granted.

Section 2. Eligibility.
(a) The Licensing Office shall grant a permit to an applicant who holds liability insurance and has passed a fire safety inspection.
(b) The Licensing Office shall deny a permit to an applicant who does not meet subsection (a).
(c) Subsection (b) does not apply to an insured applicant whose requested fire safety inspection has not yet been performed. The Licensing Office shall issue that applicant a provisional permit.

Section 3. Fees.
(a) A permit holder shall pay a permit fee of 250 dollars to the Town.
(b) A nonprofit organization is exempt from the fee in subsection (a).

Section 4. Appeals.
(a) An applicant whose application is denied may appeal to the Town Council within 30 days after the denial.
