# Meridian Electronic Claims and Receipt Act (fictional draft)

This text is an invented draft bill for the Law Machine demonstration. It has not been introduced or enacted anywhere, and Meridian is not a real jurisdiction.

Section 1. Short title.
(a) This Act may be cited as the Meridian Electronic Claims and Receipt Act.

Section 2. Definitions.
(a) "Intake system" means an electronic system designated by the Claims Commission to receive claims for all agencies.
(b) "Receipt time" means the time at which a submission first enters an intake system.

Section 3. Receipt.
(a) A submission is received at its receipt time, whether or not an employee has read it.
(b) The intake system shall ordinarily issue an electronic receipt within 60 seconds after the receipt time.
(c) A claim is presented when it has been received by the responsible agency and states a sum certain.

Section 4. Claims sent to the wrong agency.
(a) An agency that receives a claim for which it is not responsible shall route the claim to the responsible agency within 2 business days after the receipt time.
(b) A claim routed under subsection (a) is treated as received by the responsible agency at the receipt time of the original submission.
(c) A failure to route within the period in subsection (a) does not change the receipt time preserved by subsection (b).
(d) A claimant may file a complaint with the Claims Commission about an agency that fails to route a claim within the period in subsection (a).

Section 5. Missing amount.
(a) If a claim does not state a sum certain, the responsible agency shall notify the claimant of the omission within 15 business days after the receipt time.
(b) A claimant who supplies the missing amount within 30 days after the notice cures the omission, and the cured claim relates back to the original receipt time.
(c) The six-month disposition period begins when the claim states a sum certain.

Section 6. Disposition period.
(a) If the responsible agency does not make final disposition of a presented claim within six months after it is presented, the claimant may treat the failure as a final denial.

Section 7. System outages.
(a) When the intake system is unavailable, the Claims Commission shall provide an alternative means of submission.
(b) A submission made through an alternative means under subsection (a) is received when the alternative means accepts it.

Section 8. Effective dates.
(a) Sections 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7 take effect 30 days after enactment.
(b) Section 5 takes effect 180 days after enactment.
