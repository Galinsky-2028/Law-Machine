# Lakeside Library District Allocation Policy, reserve draft (fictional)

This draft revision is invented for the Law Machine demonstration. Lakeside is a fictional library district and this is not a real budget rule.

Section 1. Reserve.
(a) When the annual budget is adopted, the Treasurer shall first transfer the reserve amount from the general fund to the reserve account.

Section 2. Allocation.
(a) After the reserve transfer, the Treasurer shall allocate the branch amount from the general fund to the North Branch account and then to the South Branch account.
(b) If the general fund does not hold enough money for an allocation, that allocation is not made.

Section 3. Reports.
(a) The Treasurer shall publish an allocation report within 5 business days after the budget is adopted.
