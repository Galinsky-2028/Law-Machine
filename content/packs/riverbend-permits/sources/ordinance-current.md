# Riverbend Mobile Food Vending Ordinance (fictional)

This ordinance is invented for the Law Machine demonstration. Riverbend is a fictional town and this is not real law.

Section 1. Applications.
(a) A person who wishes to operate a mobile food vehicle shall apply to the Licensing Office.
(b) The Licensing Office shall decide a complete application within 20 business days after it is filed.

Section 2. Eligibility.
(a) The Licensing Office shall grant a permit to an applicant who holds liability insurance and has passed a fire safety inspection.
(b) The Licensing Office shall deny a permit to an applicant who does not meet subsection (a).

Section 3. Fees.
(a) A permit holder shall pay a permit fee of 250 dollars to the Town.

Section 4. Appeals.
(a) An applicant whose application is denied may appeal to the Town Council within 30 days after the denial.
