# Meridian Claims Regulation Part 14 (fictional)

This text is invented for the Law Machine demonstration. Meridian is not a real jurisdiction and none of these rules are real law.

Section 14.1. Scope.
(a) This Part governs written claims for money damages against an agency of the fictional Commonwealth of Meridian.

Section 14.2. When a claim is presented.
(a) A claim is presented when the responsible agency receives written notice of the incident accompanied by a claim for money damages in a sum certain.
(b) An agency that receives a claim for which it is not responsible shall transfer the claim to the responsible agency, if that agency can be identified, and shall notify the claimant of the transfer.
(c) A claim transferred under paragraph (b) is presented when the responsible agency receives it.

Section 14.3. Disposition period.
(a) If the responsible agency does not make final disposition of a presented claim within six months after it is presented, the claimant may treat the failure as a final denial.

Section 14.4. Missing amount.
(a) An agency that receives a claim that does not state a sum certain shall return the claim to the claimant with a statement that the amount is missing.
(b) A claim returned under paragraph (a) is presented when the responsible agency receives a claim that states a sum certain.
